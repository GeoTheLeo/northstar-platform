The NorthStar Agentic Intervention Copilot is an AI agent that finds at-risk learners and proposes interventions.

The agent works in two phases. First it writes a plan of one to six steps. Then it executes the plan by calling tools, such as listing at-risk learners or looking up one learner's details. The tools call NorthStar's real early warning and segmentation models through an HTTP API.

The agent cannot take action on its own. Its only side-effecting tool, propose intervention, adds a pending item to an approval queue. Nothing happens to a learner until a staff member reviews the proposal and approves it in the queue.

This human-in-the-loop design keeps people responsible for decisions about learners, while the agent does the investigation and drafting.
